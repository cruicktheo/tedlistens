About

I started out making mobile games at PikPok in Wellington. In London I worked on spatial data for buildings at Pupil, then on digital twins of shops at VirtualRetail.io, before spending four and a half years leading engineering at FitXR.

Now I'm technical director at Fun and Awe, a studio making immersive work at the intersection of art, play and storytelling. The studio's work is rooted in the physical world: real materials, object design and architecture, made for people to share a space and play together. Alongside OPERATOR, the studio has made SPECTRUM, a playable installation of spheres floating across a room.

The blocks at the top of this page are a nod to OPERATOR, where you compose music by arranging blocks in space. If you're reading this in a headset browser, you can step in and pick them up.
